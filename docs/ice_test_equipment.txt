List of equipment needed for ICE board testing

I marked with a star (*) the equipment that I plan to bring with me, but it 
would still be useful to have that in the lab so I can leave a working setup

1) *Gigabit ethernet switch - it is important to have a *gigabit* switch,
	otherwise it will not support jumbo frames sent by the iceboard.
2) Bunch of ethernet cables - at least four: two to connect the iceboard to the
	switch, one to connect the laptop and one to connect the switch to the
	network
3) A power supply - iceboard requires 15-20 V and consumes about 65 W.
	Usually, I set it at 17-18V.
4) *SFP connector
5) Amplifiers (if needed) with SMA interface that work in the iceboard frequency
	range and power supply for them. Iceboard's ADC work in range approximately 
	+-500 mV
6) Some attenuators with SMA interface might be useful.
7) *A desktop fan to cool the iceboard. I usually just put a basic PC fan on 
	the side - that should be enough
8) *SD card
	
I suppose that the output from dishes comes via SMA interface. Otherwise, we will
need adapters. Also, it's not required, but it would be nice to have a signal 
generation setup to test the iceboard if there will be problems with dish outputs, 
that includes:

9) Signal generator that generates a signal in a frequency range of the iceboard
10) Bunch of SMA cables (at least three)
11) A signal splitter with SMA interfaces

Also, if you have a working GPS clock with a IRIG-B Time Code Standard, we can use
that instead of a system clock to get a more accurate clock. That setup would 
include:
12) GPS clock with IRIG-B standard
13) A pair of BNC to SMA adapters (assuming the gps clock output cables have BNC  
	interface)
14) More attenuators
