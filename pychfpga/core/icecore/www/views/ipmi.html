IPMI Data

An IPMI descriptor contains information about the hardware's identity and revisions. It is stored in flash or EEPROM devices on the hardware itself. It's not psychic: an incorrectly written IPMI block will tell you worthless information; ordinarily, however, IPMI data is written during board QC and shouldn't be modified after that.

This page returns IPMI data for the motherboard and mezzanine, when it can be found. Missing entries typically indicate either the hardware is missing (e.g. for mezzanines), or that the IPMI descriptor was never written in the first place.

Most hardware's IPMI descriptors are available even prior to being powered on.
